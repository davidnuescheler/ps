The thesis

A bottle is a core sample — strata of soil, season, and the people who refused to polish it away.

Public Sediments is a small, independently owned wine dealer. We introduce authentic, terroir-focused natural wines made by growers rather than brands: organically and biodynamically farmed fruit, fermented with native yeasts, bottled without commercial additives — no powdered tannin, no coloring, no flavor kits.

Like the domaines we represent, we are independent. The list is short on purpose. If a wine cannot speak for a place, it does not ship.

Our standards

Low intervention.
High fidelity.

We buy like we drink: from independent farmers who treat the vineyard as an ecosystem, not a factory floor.

Nothing added

Most wine is built in the cellar — cultured yeast, powdered tannin, acid, enzymes, coloring. Ours is not. If it did not grow in the vineyard, it does not belong in the bottle.

Independent growers

We work with small, family-run domaines. We know how they farm, how they ferment, and why they bottle the way they do. No cooperatives in costume. No private-label programs.

Living soils

Organic at minimum; biodynamic whenever the grower has gone that far. Cover crops, compost, animals in the rows. Sediment in the glass starts as sediment in the ground.

Processed simply

Ripe fruit, native ferment, time. Unfined, unfiltered, and often without added sulfur — or with so little it is a fingerprint, not a mask.

Farming is the winemaking.

The strata

Three bottles. One layer at a time.

Each month we ship a three-bottle allocation — a white or skin-contact, a red, and a wild card — with notes on soils, vessels, and the people who made them. Limited to 200 shares.
